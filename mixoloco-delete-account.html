Delete Your Mixoloco Account

Mixoloco is developed by TheBrinkLabs. This page explains how to request deletion of your Mixoloco account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Email thebrinklabs1@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Mixoloco Account". If you signed in with Google, please mention that and the email address you used — it helps us find the right account faster. Otherwise, let us know your in-game nickname if you can.

What gets deleted

Once we process your request, we permanently delete:

  • Your profile (in-game nickname)
  • Your weekly league history, rankings, and results
  • Your credits, level, and evolution/tier progress tied to your account
  • The device identifier used for duplicate-account detection
  • The link between your account and your Google sign-in, if you used one

We don't retain any of this data after deletion. Note that this only covers data stored on our servers tied to your account — any local settings or save data on your own device are not affected and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

How long it takes

We process deletion requests within 30 days.
