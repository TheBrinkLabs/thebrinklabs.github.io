Delete Your Capitle Account

Capitle is developed by TheBrinkLabs. This page explains how to request deletion of your Capitle account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Email thebrinklabs1@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Capitle Account", from the email address (if any) linked to your account, and let us know your in-app nickname if you can. If you signed in with Google, please mention that too — it helps us find the right account faster.

What gets deleted

Once we process your request, we permanently delete:

  • Your profile (nickname, country)
  • Your league history, rankings, and weekly results
  • Your streak and score records tied to your account
  • The link between your account and your Google sign-in, if you used one

We don't retain any of this data after deletion. Note that this only covers data stored on our servers tied to your account — any local statistics saved directly on your own device are not affected and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

How long it takes

We process deletion requests within 30 days.
